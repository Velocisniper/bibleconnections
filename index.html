HOW TO PLAY:

  • Find groups of 4 words sharing a common Bible verse.
  • Select 4 words and tap "Submit" to guess.
  • You have 7 lives to solve the entire board.
  • Anchors represent a guaranteed word for each hidden verse.
  • Box colors mark Bible position (Yellow = earliest, Purple = latest).
  • Feel free to use a Bible, but don't use a search tool.
  • Use the 💡 Hint button once per game to reveal part of one of the verses.
